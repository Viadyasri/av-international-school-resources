# School Admission Questions Bank

## Questions Parents Can Ask Before Choosing a School

Choosing a school is easier when you know what information you need.

This question bank is designed for parents comparing schools in Secunderabad. Instead of relying only on rankings, brochures, or online opinions, use these questions during school visits, counselling sessions, or admission discussions.

---

## 📚 1. Academics & Curriculum

* How is the curriculum delivered in the classroom?
* Does the school focus on conceptual understanding?
* How are students supported when they struggle with a subject?
* How frequently is student progress assessed?
* Are there opportunities for advanced or additional learning?

---

## 👩‍🏫 2. Teachers & Student Support

* What qualifications and experience do teachers have?
* How accessible are teachers to students?
* How does the school provide individual attention?
* What support is available for students who need additional help?
* How does the school encourage students to ask questions?

---

## 🌱 3. Overall Student Development

* What opportunities are available outside academics?
* Are students encouraged to participate in sports and cultural activities?
* Are there leadership opportunities?
* Does the school encourage communication and public speaking?
* How are creativity and critical thinking developed?

---

## 🏫 4. Campus & Safety

* What safety measures are followed on campus?
* How are visitors and student entry managed?
* What medical support is available?
* How are school buses monitored?
* How does the school maintain classroom and campus hygiene?

---

## 🤝 5. Parent Communication

* How frequently do parents receive progress updates?
* How are parent-teacher meetings conducted?
* Who should parents contact if they have a concern?
* Does the school provide a parent communication platform?
* How does the school involve parents in a child's development?

---

## 🎯 6. Questions About Daily School Life

* What does a typical school day look like?
* How much time is allocated to extracurricular activities?
* How much homework is generally given?
* What opportunities do students have to collaborate?
* How does the school balance academics with student well-being?

---

## ⭐ 7. The Five Questions I Would Never Skip

If you have limited time during a school visit, ask these five:

1. **How does the school support a student who is struggling academically?**
2. **How do teachers encourage students to participate and ask questions?**
3. **What opportunities are available beyond academics?**
4. **How does the school communicate with parents about student progress?**
5. **What makes this learning environment different from other schools?**

## 🌟 Final Thought

The goal isn't to find the school with the longest list of facilities.

The goal is to understand whether the school matches your child's learning needs, interests, personality, and long-term development.

A well-prepared question can sometimes reveal more than a well-designed brochure.
